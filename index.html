Combat power &
fighting effectiveness

Displayed Power is only a score. Fighting effectiveness comes from the upgrades that actually change what your primary squad can survive, carry, mitigate and kill.

Build combat power, not displayed power. Every major resource spend should unlock a combat breakpoint, strengthen the primary fighting squad, or improve a permanent combat multiplier.

1Real combat power vs displayed Power

Top Force awards Power for many upgrades, but those upgrades do not all improve the same fight. Before spending, identify exactly what the upgrade affects.

Highest value

Breakpoints

Military Base gates hero levels and buildings. Barracks 24, 27 and 30 unlock stronger unit tiers. These upgrades can change several combat systems at once.

Permanent

Combat multipliers

ATK, HP, DEF, Units Attack, damage-up effects, troop-type Centers and combat research continue working every fight they apply to.

Concentrated

Primary squad

Tier, skills and scarce gear matter most when they strengthen the five heroes and units you actually send into important fights.

2Progression breakpoints

Military Base and Barracks are not ordinary building upgrades. They create combat gates. Military Base raises hero caps and unlocks prerequisites; Barracks unlocks stronger unit tiers. Push them when the next level creates a meaningful breakpoint, not merely because the building can be upgraded.

    Use this as a decision order, not a rigid recipe. A breakpoint that unlocks a new unit tier or hero cap can outrank a small percentage upgrade; otherwise keep resources concentrated on permanent combat gains and the primary squad.

    6Unit tiers are combat breakpoints

    7Resource discipline

    Resources become scarce enough that sequencing is part of combat strength. Bank toward defined breakpoints and permanent multipliers instead of spending because an upgrade button is available.

    Observed breakpoint

    Military Base 23 → 24

    Raises the hero level cap from 115 to 120 and is required for Barracks 24, which unlocks Unit Lv.8. The observed upgrade also raises Military Base Hero HP from 9,000 to 9,500. One progression step therefore opens multiple combat gains.

    Resource or itemDefault use
    Food / Iron / CoinsFund the next combat breakpoint first: Military Base prerequisites, Barracks tier unlocks, primary-type Center and direct combat research.
    Speed-upsSave for meaningful breakpoints and scoring events wherever practical.
    Hero shardsConcentrate on heroes that actually occupy the primary fighting squad or unlock a specific synergy. Do not spend merely to raise account Power.
    Skill medalsPrioritize high-impact PvP skills: team buffs, enemy debuffs, carry damage and frontline mitigation.
    Gear materialsScarce. Concentrate them where the next upgrade creates the strongest tank, carry or team-wide breakpoint; do not level every slot evenly by habit.
    Training itemsNew unit tiers, combat preparation, and high-value event scoring.
    DiamondsAvoid convenience spending that creates no permanent or strategic power.

    Get paid twice for the same upgrade

    Alliance Duel scores research power and research speed-ups on Tech day; hero shards, skill medals, and Hero EXP on Hero day; building power, research power, training, and several speed-up types on War Preparations. Where timing allows, spend inside the window that rewards that exact action — without delaying critical combat progression indefinitely.

    8Build the squad you have

    This guide does not recommend specific heroes. Hero availability, spending, events and roster luck vary too much for a universal shopping list. Use your best available roster to build one coherent primary fighting squad, then invest according to combat function.

    1 · Function

    Give every slot a job

    Build around survival, damage and support. Front-row heroes need to stay alive; damage dealers need enough protection to keep dealing damage; buffs and debuffs should improve the whole formation rather than exist only to raise Power.

    2 · Scope

    Read the skill text

    Effects that work in general combat, protect the front row, buff the squad or weaken enemy heroes can improve PvP. Effects restricted to Monsters, gathering or guarding inside the base should not be counted as general field-PvP strength.

    3 · Investment

    Concentrate before expanding

    Keep the primary five near the available level cap and direct scarce shards, skill materials and gear toward meaningful combat breakpoints. Build secondary heroes after the primary squad can do the job you need it to do.

    QuestionWhat to look for
    Does this skill work in PvP?Look for general damage, mitigation, ATK/DEF/HP changes, team buffs, enemy debuffs, cooldown effects and other wording that is not restricted to Monsters, gathering or base defense.
    Does it help the formation?Whole-squad effects, front-row protection, carry protection and useful enemy debuffs can create more fighting value than a larger individual Power number.
    What does the next tier/star unlock?Prioritize breakpoints that materially change damage, mitigation, duration, target count, effect scope or passive combat stats.
    Does the hero fit the troop-type plan?Formation bonuses, troop-type Center investment, research and counters all matter. Do not force a hero solely because of rarity.
    Should I invest now?Only if the hero is part of the primary fighting plan or the investment unlocks a specific combat advantage. Account Power alone is not enough.

    Hero rule: the guide teaches how to evaluate the roster you already have. Rarity and displayed Power are inputs, not a prescription for which heroes to buy.

    9Research: rank the effect, not the Power reward

    Research is permanent, but the tree mixes field PvP, conditional defense, progression, economy and PvE. Rank the effect first; use the displayed Power reward only as a secondary number.

    10Fighting accounts richer than you

    A larger spender can buy breadth, acceleration and replacement capacity. The practical counter is efficiency: concentrate permanent combat gains, reach troop and hero breakpoints sooner, use counters intelligently, coordinate fights, and refuse bad exchanges.

    Do

    • Concentrate five strong heroes into one primary formation.
    • Maintain the highest practical unit tier; Barracks 24/27/30 are major power gates.
    • Use troop-type counters and damage-profile counters when the strength gap is manageable.
    • Fight with alliance concentration, rallies, reinforcement, and timing.
    • Force the whale to spend teleports, stamina, healing, shields, time and replacement resources for limited gain.
    • Preserve troops when the fight is unwinnable.

    Avoid

    • Leveling every hero or research node because it raises displayed Power.
    • Producing enormous numbers of obsolete troops.
    • Attacking a whale only because your troop type counters theirs.
    • Feeding individual marches into a stronger account.
    • Giving repeatable easy kills or undefended objectives.
    • Treating every attack as a mandatory stand-and-fight.

    11The combat-power loop

    Run this in order and repeat it. The loop is built to keep resources pointed at real PvP gains instead of account-padding upgrades.
