    6Resource discipline

    Late upgrades are expensive enough that bad sequencing becomes a combat problem. The direct Military Base 22 upgrade runs about 114.1M Food + 114.1M Iron + 38M Coins — 266.2M total. From a minimally prepared Lv. 21 account the full path can approach 477.5M. Commit resources to a defined breakpoint or event objective, not to whichever button is lit.

    Resource or itemDefault use
    Food / Iron / CoinsMilitary Base prerequisites, primary type Center, Barracks breakpoints, critical research.
    Speed-upsSave for meaningful breakpoints and scoring events wherever practical.
    Hero shardsPrimary five-hero march first. Avoid a collection of half-built heroes.
    Skill medalsPrimary combat skills first.
    Gear materialsFinish strong sets on primary heroes before spreading upgrades.
    Training itemsNew unit tiers, combat preparation, and high-value event scoring.
    DiamondsAvoid convenience spending that creates no permanent or strategic power.

    Get paid twice for the same upgrade

    Alliance Duel scores research power and research speed-ups on Tech day; hero shards, skill medals, and Hero EXP on Hero day; building power, research power, training, and several speed-up types on War Preparations. Where timing allows, spend inside the window that rewards that exact action — without delaying critical combat progression indefinitely.

    7Fighting accounts richer than you

    You do not beat a whale by imitating one. Their advantage is purchased breadth, acceleration, and replacement capacity. The counter is to maximise the combat value of every resource and deny them favourable exchanges.

    Do

    • Concentrate five strong heroes into one primary formation.
    • Maintain the highest practical unit tier.
    • Use counters when the strength gap is manageable.
    • Fight with alliance concentration, rallies, reinforcement, and timing.
    • Force the whale to spend teleports, stamina, healing, and time for limited gain.
    • Preserve troops when the fight is unwinnable.

    Avoid

    • Leveling every hero because it raises account power.
    • Producing enormous numbers of obsolete troops.
    • Attacking a whale only because your troop type counters theirs.
    • Feeding individual marches into a stronger account.
    • Giving repeatable easy kills or undefended objectives.
    • Treating every attack as a mandatory stand-and-fight.
