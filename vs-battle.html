ALLIANCE DUEL · WEEKLY VS

Win the week.
Not every day.

The duel awards 13 Victory Points across six days. Seven locks the weekly win. The strategy is therefore simple: spend enough to secure the days that matter, save resources that can score again later, and arrive at Day 6 with enough left if those four points are needed.

THE WEEKLY PLAN

Save what you can until you know which days we need

Day 1 is cheap to surrender. Days 2–5 are useful two-point fights. Day 6 is the four-point SVS Kill Event. How much we spend should depend on the weekly score. Do not use more than we need to win a day. If the week is already won, save troops and resources for the next objective instead of fighting just because the event is open.

Day 5 · War Preparations

Building, tech, training and radar all score on Day 5. Use what is needed to win the two points. If the day is already won, stop spending and save the rest for Day 6 or next week.

4 VP

Day 6 · SVS Kill Event

Four points can decide the week. If we need the four points, use the truce option or the established kill-squad plan. If we already have enough Victory Points to win the week, shield and save troops for Capitol or the next fight.

If ahead: use only what we need to keep the lead. Save everything else.
If close: use items that only score today first. Use items that also score on later days only if we need them to win today.
If badly behind: do not use everything trying to save a 1- or 2-point day. Check whether saving those resources gives us a better chance to reach 7 VP later in the week.

What we should do

DAY 6 · KILL EVENT

Fight as cells. Score troops. DON’T ZERO.

During Day 6 the matched servers can teleport across servers and attack bases. FAM uses coordinated five-player strike cells rather than sending players out alone.

OPTION 1 · DIPLOMACY

Contact the opposing R4 / R5

Before KE, our R4/R5 can contact the opposing alliance R4/R5 and offer a truce. If both sides agree, we shield, avoid attacking each other, and save troops and resources.

R4 / R5 announces whether we have a truce or are fighting. Members follow that call.
OPTION 2 · COMBAT

Deploy 3–4 strike cells

Each cell travels together. The two strongest players attack while three capable support players remain shielded and protect the exposed attackers.

DON’T ZERO

Score troops, not bases.

KE points come from troop kills. Watch the scoring during the attack. When the target stops producing troop-kill points, stop hitting it and move to another scoring target. Do not continue attacking simply to zero the base.

WHAT TO SAVE

Know which days each resource scores

Some items score on only one day. Others score again later in the week. Use the one-day items on their scoring day. Save multi-day items when possible until we know which day we need them to win.

SCORES ON ONE DAY

Save it for that day

Hero Shards, Skill Medals, Valor Badges and Airship Component Chests should be saved during the week and used on the day that gives VS points for them.

SCORES ON MORE THAN ONE DAY

Do not use it too early

Building, tech and training speed-ups score on multiple days. Use them today only when we need the points; otherwise save them for a later day.

DAY 6

Keep something left if KE may decide the week

UR Trucks, UR Covert Ops, healing speed-ups and unused building, tech and training speed-ups can all score on Day 6. Save some when Day 6 may be needed. If the week is already won, there is no reason to burn them for VS.
